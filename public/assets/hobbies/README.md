# Hobby card image assets

These images are used by the homepage's four-card hobby row.

| Asset | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `cube-3d.webp` | Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool; transparent 3D cube with violet and blue light trails. |
| `fitness-3d.webp` | Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool; transparent 3D dumbbells with copper and violet lighting. |
| `league-icon.webp` | Official League of Legends rendered game icon, resized from `LoL_Icon_Rendered_Hi-Res.png`. |

The final site assets are stored in `assets/hobbies/` (or `dist/assets/hobbies/` in the local project). Their alpha channels are preserved. CSS adds glow, orbit, particle and hover effects, with reduced-motion support.

Official League of Legends asset source: https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/league-of-legends-downloads/

Official icon package: https://cmsassets.rgpub.io/sanity/files/dsfx7636/news/443020e6faac361cd44ced1c12108e4de2e60ef9.zip

## Final prompts

### Cube

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a luxury 3D raster icon for the Rubik’s Cube hobby card on a refined academic portfolio. Subject: one mechanically plausible 3x3 Rubik’s cube, three visible faces, slightly turned top layer, beveled glossy enamel tiles, dark graphite inner core, jewel-like purple, sapphire blue and turquoise faces with a few classic warm accents. Camera: three-quarter isometric product view, full cube centered, generous clear margins, object occupies about 65% of a square frame. Detailed physically based materials, crisp dimensional edges, cinematic studio lighting, violet-blue emissive rim light, subtle luminous orbital trail and a few tiny sparkling particles close around the cube. Realistic luxury 3D render with depth and polished reflections, impressive and elegant rather than a line drawing. Isolated on a truly transparent background, soft floating contact shadow only, no background panel. Readable at 240 pixels. No text, no numbers, no watermark, no extra cubes, no people, no typography. Output one square transparent PNG.

### Fitness

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a luxury 3D raster icon for the Fitness hobby card on a refined academic portfolio, matching a jewel-toned 3D cube icon. Subject: two premium adjustable dumbbells in a compact diagonal crossed composition, anatomically recognizable weights, knurled chrome grips, substantial polished dark titanium plates with warm copper edges and small violet reflections. Camera: three-quarter isometric product view, complete objects centered with generous clear margins, subject occupies about 70% of a square frame. Detailed physically based metal, studio reflections, amber and violet rim lighting, a restrained luminous energy arc around the weights, several tiny gold glints, a soft floating shadow. High-end cinematic 3D product render, richly dimensional and elegant, not cartoon line art, not a gym scene. Isolated on a truly transparent background, no backdrop panel. Readable at 240 pixels. No text, no letters, no watermark, no people, no gym machinery. Output one square transparent PNG.
